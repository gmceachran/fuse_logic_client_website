Who we are

Where aviation meets technology

Every aircraft transaction depends on proving what the aircraft is made of and where each controlled part came from. That proof is still largely assembled by hand, at enormous cost in time.

That's the work we take off your plate. You send us your records as they are, and we send them back organized, reviewed and ready for your transaction.

Fuselagic brings together aviation industry relationships and a team experienced in building software and data systems. Our methods were developed on real-world records packages provided through industry partners.
