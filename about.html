About FuseLogic

Aviation records, approached as a data problem.

Contact Us

Bringing two industries together

FuseLogic was founded to bring the aviation and technology sectors together on one of the industry's most manual jobs: assembling and reviewing aircraft records. Every purchase, sale, lease and return requires proving what an aircraft is made of, and that proof is still largely assembled page by page.

Our team pairs aviation industry relationships with experience building software and data systems. Our methods were developed on real-world records packages provided through industry partners, including the landing gear records of an Airbus A330.
